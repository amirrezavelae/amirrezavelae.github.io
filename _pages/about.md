<!-- Introduction -->
<section id="about-formal">
  <h2>Formal Bio</h2>
  <hr style="margin-bottom: 20px;">
  <p>
    My name is Amirreza Velae. I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering, with a minor in Applied Mathematics, from Sharif University of Technology, and I am currently an M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at the <a href="https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/" target="_blank">Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)</a>, working with the <a href="https://sanctusfactory.com/u-aim/" target="_blank"> U-AIM Lab </a> under  <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gFWgUQEAAAAJ&hl=en"> Prof. Chang D. Yoo</a>. 
    My primary research interest is reinforcement learning, which I view as a promising framework for advancing toward general intelligence. I am especially drawn to the theoretical foundations of deep reinforcement learning and control.
  </p>

  <p>
    For my B.Sc. thesis, I studied the numerical optimization behind Trust Region Policy Optimization (TRPO) under the supervision of <a href="https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=dcjIFccAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Prof. Hamed Shah-Mansouri</a>. 
    With <a href="https://arashbaharik.github.io/" target="_blank">Arash Bahari Kordabad</a> and <a href="https://hycodev.com/ssoudjani" target="_blank">Prof. Sadegh Soudjani</a> at the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems, I contributed to second-order policy-gradient methods for the Linear Quadratic Regulator, deriving an exact Hessian expression and Newton-type updates for the discounted stochastic setting. This work was published in <em>Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence</em>.
    I also collaborated with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=yiZk6coAAAAJ&hl=en" target="_blank">Prof. Mohammad Aliannejadi</a> at the University of Amsterdam on debiasing retrieval systems, converting Backpack language models into encoders to mitigate gender bias in ranking; this work was accepted to <strong>ECIR 2026</strong>.
  </p>
</section>
